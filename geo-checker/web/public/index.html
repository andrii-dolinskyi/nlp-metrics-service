Three simple questions decide if AI quotes your page

Think of AI tools as very picky editors. Before they quote a page in an answer, they check three things. Our scanner checks the same three — with 50+ individual signals.

Can AI even open your page?

Every AI tool sends a robot to read your site. If your site tells those robots to go away (a file called robots.txt controls this), your page is invisible to AI — no matter how good the content is. We check the door policy for 11 different AI robots.

2

Is it easy to quote?

AI answers are short. They lift small, self-contained pieces: a direct answer under a heading, a table row, a statistic with a source, an FAQ entry. Pages built from long unstructured text rarely get picked. We measure how liftable your page is.

FAQ

Common questions

What is "AI visibility"?

When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google AI a question, the answer often quotes a few web pages as sources. AI visibility means your pages are the ones being quoted. It is the new version of "ranking on Google" — except instead of ten blue links, there is usually one answer with two or three sources.

Is this the same as SEO?

It overlaps, but it is not the same. Classic SEO gets you into a list of links. AI tools pull out small passages and prefer direct answers, verified facts with named sources, machine-readable structure, and human-sounding writing. A page can rank #1 on Google and still never be quoted by ChatGPT. This practice is often called GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) or AEO (Answer Engine Optimization).

How is the score calculated?

100 points across seven categories — page structure, proof and sources, machine-readable data, AI robot access, freshness, and two writing-quality categories. Every check is deterministic: the same page always gets the same score. No AI guessing is involved in the scoring itself.

What is a good score?

80+ means the page is genuinely well prepared for AI search — few pages on the internet get there. 70–79 is solid. Below 55, AI tools are mostly skipping the page. Most business pages we scan land between 40 and 65, so even a few fixes can put you ahead of competitors.

Why does "sounding human" matter to an AI?

Two reasons. AI search tools are trained to prefer original, trustworthy content — and machine-written text signals neither. And human readers bounce off robotic text too, which feeds back into every ranking system. Our scanner detects the same patterns readers subconsciously notice: repetitive rhythm, filler phrases, hype words, "not just X, it's Y" constructions.

My score is low. What now?

My site blocks AI robots. Is that bad?

It is a choice, but know what it costs: if GPTBot or PerplexityBot is blocked in your robots.txt, your content cannot appear in those tools' answers at all. Many sites blocked AI crawlers in 2023–24 by default and never revisited the decision — while their competitors became the sources AI recommends.
