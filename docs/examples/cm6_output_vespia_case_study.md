# How Eurora verified 1,200 business customers in 100 countries with Vespia

Eurora Solutions automates customs and tax compliance for online sellers. With Vespia, it verified more than 1,200 business customers operating in more than 100 countries, and it reports a verification cost 37 times lower than market prices. ## Who Eurora is and why every seller must be verified

Eurora is a cross-border trade compliance platform. Because it handles tax and customs on behalf of its business customers, it has to know exactly who each customer is. Marko Lastik founded the Estonian company in 2018. Its AI platform calculates duties and taxes and assigns Harmonised System (HS) codes. It also screens goods for restrictions and clears them through customs for online sellers and marketplaces. Logistics and postal companies use these services too. Eurora was the first official VAT intermediary and the first company to issue an Import One-Stop Shop (IOSS) number. In April 2022 it raised a [$40 million Series A](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/04/27/2430286/0/en/eurora-solutions-raises-40m-in-series-a-to-tackle-e-commerce-shipping-delays-caused-by-eu-regulation-changes.html) led by Connected Capital. At the time, that was one of the largest Series A rounds in Estonia.

That funding came with regulatory pressure attached. Shaffy Roell, Investment Manager at Connected Capital, said at the close of the round: "We have seen a clear push from regulators to improve transparency and reporting for the increasing number of goods that enter through customs." An intermediary that files VAT for a seller takes on responsibility for that seller's filings. So a fake company or a sanctioned owner becomes the intermediary's problem as well.

Before a compliance provider onboards a seller, it checks four things, in this order:

1. The company exists and is active in its home commercial register.
2. The ultimate beneficial owners (UBOs), who are the people who own or control the company, are identified through every layer of ownership.
3. The company and its people are clear of politically exposed person (PEP) lists and sanctions lists. They are also clear of adverse media.
4. The person who opens the account is who they say they are, and they're authorised to act for the company.

## The problem: UBO documents from dozens of sources

Before Vespia, Eurora's compliance team confirmed each customer's UBOs by collecting documents from several separate sources. That meant manual work and waiting time on every onboarding. This work is necessary because every country runs its own register, and those registers publish different data in different languages and formats. Some list shareholders and some don't.

UBO verification gets harder when ownership is layered. Take a seller registered in one country and owned by a holding company in another. To reach the real owner, you need a second register and a third. Each extract has to be requested before it can be read and compared with the others. Then someone decides whether the records agree.

That work grows with every new customer, and it's where mistakes creep in. Extracts go out of date, or two sources name different directors. If you're assessing your own process, count the sources a single cross-border file needs. That number tells you how much of the work is collection and how much is judgement.

## The solution: KYB, UBO, AML and KYC checks in one platform

Vespia gave Eurora one platform that combines know your business (KYB) checks with UBO verification. Anti-money laundering (AML) and know your customer (KYC) checks run on the same platform. UBO information now arrives in seconds from a single place, where it once came from tens of sources. The two sub-questions below are the ones buyers ask first.

### How long a company and UBO check takes

An automated KYB check on Vespia returns in under 30 seconds. It draws on commercial register data from more than 300 jurisdictions and over 5,000 official sources, and it returns more than 1,500 data points per check. After the data arrives, customers in jurisdictions that keep ownership data unpublished still have to supply documents. The full onboarding time depends on that slowest step.

### How screening and identity checks join the same flow

The company and its people are screened against more than 4,000 lists in the same run. Alongside PEP and sanctions lists, these include adverse media lists. Identity verification covers over 7,000 document types from more than 190 countries, so the account holder is checked in the same file as the company. Before you choose any provider, check that its register coverage matches the home countries of your actual customers. A headline coverage figure won't tell you that.

## What Eurora's compliance team does differently now

Eurora's compliance team now starts each onboarding from a completed file that replaces the ownership picture it previously built by hand. The team spends far less effort on checking whether records agree than it did on finding them. Eurora says it's satisfied with the smooth KYB and UBO checks on its all-in-one platform. Automation surfaces the data, while reviewers still exercise judgement on high-risk files. A seller with a sanctions near-match or an opaque holding structure is one example. For a team that wants automated KYB onboarding, the practical test is how exceptions reach reviewers. So ask any provider how it flags a file for manual review and what the reviewer sees when it does.
